# VOLCANIC ISLAND — Independent review

Assessment by an agent distinct from the designer, from the guide, the numerical definition, the table of points and the plan. The corrected version was reviewed after opening the valley curves and adding the tunnel spurs.

## Verdict

**Coherent proposal for a first mock-up. Closed geometry verified in plan and profile; driving and 3D terrain not tested.** The descent remains a sector of anticipation and braking, not a promise of continuous full throttle. The remaining reservations are mock-up checks, with no conceptual obstacle to this proposal.

## Objections addressed

### East descent

The first version combined a 94.9 m radius and a −14.0% slope at PK 3,958, after an almost straight passage. This combination encouraged too fast an arrival. The valley lobes were opened: the radius at the same PK rises to about 164 m, and the global minimum reaches 142 m. This removes the most problematic tightening without turning the valley into hairpins.

**Prescription to keep:** 14 m roadway, target clear view of 180 m on the descending curves, announcement of the manoeuvre about 150 m before, houses and vegetation set back from the inside of the corners. The slope still reaches −15.29% at PK 4,070, with a radius of about 258 m: the spectacle is accepted, but a braking/drift sequence must be clearly identified. The visibility distances are composition targets to be confirmed with the camera and game speed, not certified stopping distances.

### Tunnels

The intervals PK 1,600–1,800 and 2,180–2,320 now cross two spurs shown on the plan. They are located on wide curves and on the main climb. The first pierces a cliff nose; the second crosses a tongue of consolidated rock before the pass. Their presence is geographically motivated.

**Prescription to keep:** continuity of the 14 m roadway in the tunnels, 1 m side margins, perceptible rock cover, exits extended by a full road ledge. The bright opening must reveal the next road and never suggest an exit into the void. The interior visibility reservation may require clearance greater than the 5 m envisaged depending on the real position of the camera and walls: check in volume.

### Documents

The start/finish label and the valley mention were moved; their collisions with marker 6 and the road have disappeared. The arrows and six sectors remain readable. The file must complete the numbers with the driving intent: acceleration at the port, fast cornice curves, preparation of the descending S, coastal re-acceleration.

## Originality

The port–climb–panorama–descent skeleton is shared with Aegean Cliffs. The distinction rests on the long basalt cornices, the two spur tunnels, the bare pass under an imposing cone and architecture limited to small groups in the valley. The volcano must remain identifiable without the houses; no monumental square or white town must become the main signature.

Against Chapada, keep subtropical vegetation in gardens and sheltered folds; no canopy, ford, temple or signature large waterfall. Against Karasu-Tōge, maintain the continuous ascent and stretched curves. A localised fumarole and a solidified lava field suffice to sign the volcanism; no diffuse lava across the whole landscape is necessary.

## Explicit limits

Continuity is achieved in the coordinates. The relief bands remain a schema of masses, not a verified altimetric surface: they do not yet prove that the roadway is supported everywhere or that the tunnels remain covered. The first mock-up will need to verify the ledges cut into the basalt, the retaining walls, the tunnel cover, the pass and the real opening of the valley toward the sea.

Practicable speed, drift/nitro balance, views from the camera and opponent driving remain to be validated in the engine. The design document does not certify them.
