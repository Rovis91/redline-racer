Volcanic Island / La Couronne Brisée

Mood and reference sheet, V2, second edition. Rewritten after the author drove the first V2: the island moves from a subtropical to a subarctic setting (Svalbard, Iceland, Lofoten, Greenland), the galleries are replaced by open cuttings, and the zones are described as rebuilt in src/maps/volcan/ (6.58 km, run anticlockwise with the sea on the right; the layout is unchanged).

A fictional volcanic island in the far north Atlantic: black lava, grey-green moss, snow on the heights, and one small fishing town painted in every bright colour there is. The race runs on the old service road of the volcano observers: out of the harbour, up the moss slope, round the black needle at the pass, through the broken crown of the crater, and back down to the quay by the hairpins. Colour belongs to people; everything else is black, moss and white.

Place
One island, cold sea all round, a few icebergs and black stacks off the roadless coast. A closed crater in the middle whose rim is high and white on the east and west and low at two gaps where the road passes. A basalt needle at the north-east pass. The only town on the south shore.
Time and light
Late summer afternoon at high latitude: low clear sun from the south-west, long shadows, a pale cold sky, haze on the sea horizon. Snow lies from about 250 m up and in hollows lower down.
Style
Retro arcade drift, PS1 / Saturn: faceted low-poly, 64 to 128 px textures in nearest filtering, flat readable surfaces. Read at 150 km/h before being read up close.
The volcano
Quiet, watched, not erupting. Liquid lava in one place only, the small lake on the crater floor. Its rock has no stripes: large planes of one dark tone, broken by snow and moss.
Anti-references
Palms, banana groves, vines, terraces, tiled stucco houses (the first V2); a lone harbour crane; rock textures in bands; a crater rim in teeth or bent by the road; tunnels; lava everywhere, red sky; a resort.

ZONE 1Le Phare: boathouses and fish racks

PK 0 to 690, 12 to 40 m. The start leaves the quay eastwards past the working end of the harbour: red boathouses with their doors on the water side, racks of drying fish, trawlers at anchor, then bare black rock as the road begins to climb.

Mood
A working fishing station at the edge of the map of the world: cold air, tar, salt fish, bright paint on wet wood.
Landmark
The line of red boathouses on the left, gable to the road, and the tall A-frames of the fish racks on the quay.
Read at speed
  • boathouses in one colour, falu red, white corner boards, dark roofs
  • timber racks in a row, taller than a house, pale grey wood
  • trawlers and open boats in bright bands on dark water
  • red-and-white beacons on the quay edge
  • no tree at all; black rock and a little moss
Light
Low, clear subarctic sun from the south-west, long shadows, a cold pale sky; colours are saturated only on what is painted.
Transition
The buildings stop at once; the quay narrows to a ledge of black rock and the moss slope of Les Cendres opens ahead, the volcano above it with snow on its rim.
Not this
A crane or container gear; a marina; palms or any subtropical planting.

ZONE 2Les Cendres: the moss slope

PK 690 to 2210, 40 to 177 m, climbed. A long run of S-bends up an old lava and ash slope covered in grey-green moss: black boulders, stone cairns that mark the old track, two small farms with turf-dark cabins and sheep fences, snow lying in the hollows higher up. The sea is always on the right.

Mood
Empty and enormous: a road, a line of cairns, and moss as far as the snow.
Landmark
The volcano, seen whole on the left with its white rim and its broken crown; nearer, the row of cairns along the inside of the bends.
Read at speed
  • moss ground: one grey-green, lumpy, with black rock showing through
  • cairns: man-high piles of black stones, one every 150 m
  • isolated black boulders, never in rows
  • two farms: three or four small coloured cabins, a fence, nothing else
  • snow patches in the hollows from 120 m up, white with a blue shadow side
Light
Wide open; the low sun rakes the slope and every boulder throws a long shadow. Sea glitter on the right.
Transition
Moss thins to bare red-brown scoria and snow; the black needle rises ahead: the pass.
Not this
Terraces, vines or any farming in rows; stepped black walls; trees; a green alpine meadow.

ZONE 3La Couronne: the horseshoe round the needle

PK 2210 to 3780, 177 to 232 to 200 m. The signature corner: a wide horseshoe round a 105 m basalt needle and its scree, the pass at 232 m with the belvedere deck and the observation post on the sea side, snow fields, red scoria, then the road turns its back on the sea and heads for the low point of the crater rim.

Mood
Monumental and windy: one black spike, snow, a broken white-rimmed crater behind it, and half the ocean under the guardrail.
Landmark
The black needle inside the bend, the crater rim directly behind it on the approach.
Read at speed
  • a single dark spire, faceted, isolated, never on the roadway
  • snow fields with hard edges on charcoal and red-brown ground
  • the belvedere: a pale concrete deck, a railing, three benches, an antenna on the post
  • the outer flank of the volcano, smooth and white-topped, well clear of the road
  • black sea stacks and a few icebergs far below, off the roadless north coast
Light
The highest and coldest light of the lap: pale sky, white snow, black rock; the concrete of the post is the only warm tone.
Transition
Leaving the horseshoe the road runs into a shallow cutting through the lowest gap of the rim; the walls rise on both sides and the crater opens beyond.
Not this
A needle that hides the next corner; lava on the surface here; a visitor centre with a car park.

ZONE 4Le Cratère: through the broken crown

PK 3780 to 4890, 200 to 112 m. No tunnel: the crater rim is high and snow-covered on the east and west, and drops to two low gaps, north and south-west. The road goes in by the north gap in an open cutting, crosses the crater floor past a small lava lake, cooled crust, a spatter cone and fumaroles, and leaves by the south-west gap in a second cutting.

Mood
Inside the mountain under an open sky: black walls, snow above, steam, and one orange eye of lava beside the road.
Landmark
The lava lake on the left of the road in the middle of the floor; above it the white crest of the east rim.
Read at speed
  • a smooth ring of dark walls, white on top, lower ahead and behind where the road passes
  • one small orange surface with black rafts of crust
  • matt black crust and scattered boulders on the other side of the road
  • thin pale columns of steam, a red-brown spatter cone
  • the cuttings: sloping rock banks on both sides, basalt columns at their mouths
Light
The floor is in the shadow of the west rim; the east wall and its snow are in full sun. The lake is the only warm light.
Transition
The south-west cutting opens on the flank: sudden sea, sun and the coloured town 100 m below.
Not this
A tunnel or a gallery; a crater whose rim is cut into teeth or bent by the road; lava anywhere else on the map; a red sky or an ash plume.

ZONE 5Les Lacets: the hairpins above the town

PK 4890 to 6100, 112 to 12 m. Three hairpins down the south-west flank: a few coloured cabins between the legs, the white wooden church and its spire outside the second hairpin, fish racks, cairns at the bends, moss and black rock, the town getting bigger at every turn.

Mood
Coming home: the first colours after the crater, and the whole harbour laid out below.
Landmark
The white wooden church with its dark spire outside the second hairpin: the braking mark of the descent.
Read at speed
  • cabins as small vivid cubes, each a different colour, dark roofs
  • white church, pointed spire, alone on its turn
  • a cairn at each hairpin
  • moss slope with black boulders, no planting
  • the town, the quay and the lighthouse visible below from each leg
Light
South-west slope in full low sun: the warmest light of the lap, on cold colours.
Transition
The last hairpin lands on the quay: the road flattens, widens, and the coloured fronts line the left.
Not this
Banana groves, palms, gardens; terraces; a second village competing with the harbour.

ZONE 6Les Quais: the coloured town

PK 6100 to the line, 12 m. The home straight along the quay: three rows of wooden houses stepping up the slope on the island side, every one a different, very bright colour; on the quay the fish market, racks, crates and nets; boats in the basin, the breakwater and the lighthouse at its end.

Mood
A polar town painted against the dark: red, yellow, blue, green, orange, each house its own.
Landmark
The wall of coloured gables on the left for the whole straight; ahead to the right, the red-and-white lighthouse.
Read at speed
  • about twenty-five small gabled timber houses, no two neighbours the same colour
  • white window frames and corner boards, dark pitched roofs
  • houses on short stone feet, set in rows, gaps between them
  • the long low fish market on the sea side, nets and crates
  • the lighthouse on the breakwater, small boats in the basin
Light
Low sun from the right over the water, straight onto the fronts: the most saturated colours of the map.
Transition
Past the line the houses give way to the red boathouses and racks of Le Phare: the lap closes.
Not this
A lone crane; stucco houses with tiled roofs; pastel colours; a tall hotel; a street narrowed by buildings on both sides.
