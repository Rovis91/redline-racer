Sky Pillars

Mood and reference sheet, V2. Written after the blockout was validated; it follows the zones of the built map (src/maps/piliers/, relief in scenery-piliers.js), not the wording of the bible, and keeps the bible's art direction. Chainages (PK) are metres along the 4 042 m lap.

A fictional service road through sandstone pillars inspired by Zhangjiajie (Hunan, China), early in the morning. The lap climbs out of a forest, wraps two towers on a ledge, crosses the only gap on a suspension bridge, goes through a rock spur on the high plateau and comes back down a buttress above the cloud. Vertigo is the thing you drive: the rock is always close on one side and the void open on the other.

Place
Three rock shoulders round a deep ravine, floor 150 to 225 m under the road. Six towers rise from them (P1 to P5, 280 to 370 m) and one needle stands alone in the middle of the ravine (HERO, 410 m): the compass of the lap. A flat sea of clouds lies 70 m under the lowest road.
Time and light
Early morning, warm and even. Cream side light on the rock, pale powder-blue sky, cool blue-grey in the voids. The mist stays far below the road: never on an apex, never at the vanishing point.
Style
Retro low-poly, 1995 to 1998 console racing: faceted volumes, pixel textures, flat readable colours. Read at 150 km/h before being read up close.
Rock
The point raised on the blockout: the terrain is too smooth for what it stands for. This sandstone is stacked: horizontal beds 3 to 8 m thick, vertical joints that cut the towers into square-edged columns, ledges where pines and moss hold, a darker foot. Every wall near the road must show beds and joints as facets, not as a gradient.
Road works
Small against the rock and all of one family: grey-cream dressed stone. A low stone parapet on the void side, a masonry retaining wall under the road where the ledge is built out, a cut face on the rock side. They are objects placed on a simple terrain, as in the references, not painted on it.
Anti-references
Floating islands or rock with no foot; Japanese torii, giant pagodas, several viaducts; a glass-floor footbridge used as the road bridge; the natural arch of Tianmen used as a road tunnel; fog that hides a bend; photoreal or PBR rock; smooth dune-like slopes; warm desert canyon colours (this rock is sage grey under green, not red).

ZONE 1 · PK 0 to 735 and 3 700 to the lineForêt: the low rock forest

Start at +150 m on a wide, almost flat road, long easy bends, then a smooth climb to +182 m along the feet of P1 and P2. The last 340 m of the lap run through the same woods: the forest is both the start and the finish. Big trunks stand right at the barrier, one side then the other every 35 m.

Mood
Enclosed and green: you slalom between trunks under a canopy, and the mountain only shows in slices between the trees.
Landmark
The summit of P1, then P2, pale above the treetops on the right; on the last straight, the start gantry between two walls of trees.
Read at speed
  • dark trunks close to the road, alternating sides: the rhythm of the zone
  • angular pines in spaced silhouettes, dense broadleaf undergrowth at ground level
  • an 8 m bank on the inside with grey faceted boulders, moss on top
  • one or two old stone markers at the changes of rhythm
  • first open view at PK 640: the trees stop, the cliff edge comes to 14 m from the barrier
Light
Cream light from the side, strong green, forest shadow as flat blocks across the road. Mist only below the shoulder and far away.
Transition
The undergrowth thins, bare rock takes over, the first stone parapet appears on the left: the corniches begin.
Not this
A tree on the racing line or hiding an apex; a jungle (palms, lianas); a village or any large building.

ZONE 2 · PK 735 to 1 710Corniches: the ledge round the two towers

The road turns right round P2 with the void on the left, runs through a 10 m rock cut, then wraps P3 the other way: wall on the left, the notch of the ravine on the right. +182 to +215 m, 16 m wide: the drift section.

Mood
Exposed and mineral: a ledge cut into a giant wall, the car between rock and air.
Landmark
The wall of P3 filling one side of the screen for 400 m; at the end, the bridge towers seen over the last bend.
Read at speed
  • a striated sandstone wall rising from 12 m beyond the barrier to far above the frame
  • a continuous low stone parapet on the void side, cream against the blue
  • masonry retaining walls under the road where the ledge is built out
  • the wall changes side at PK 1 250 (P2 on the right, P3 on the left): the cue for the direction change
  • rare pines on the open side, off the critical angles; needles aligned in the distance
Light
Low light raking the horizontal beds: warm cream on the rock, pale blue in the void, the road in the wall's shadow for short stretches only.
Transition
Vegetation clears, the road straightens; the bridge anchor blocks are seen first, then the towers and cables.
Not this
A cantilevered walkway used as the road; steel crash barriers; a wall with no foot; rockfall on the lane.

ZONE 3 · PK 1 710 to 2 181Pont: the suspension bridge

One straight 250 m deck at +215 m over the only gap between the shoulders, a tower at each end, 215 m of air under the wheels, needles standing in the void on both sides.

Mood
A breath: no steering, full throttle, and the whole ravine opens at once.
Landmark
The far tower and its anchor block, in the axis of the deck from the moment the car straightens.
Read at speed
  • two dark steel towers, a portal each: they count the distance left
  • main cables sagging to the deck at mid-span, hangers at a regular low-density rhythm
  • massive stone anchor blocks set in the rock at both ends, same level, no gap in the barrier
  • needles rising through the cloud left and right, their feet lost
  • HERO to the right, alone and taller than everything
Light
The brightest moment of the lap: open powder-blue sky, cables as dark silhouettes, white-blue void.
Transition
After the east anchor block rock and plateau vegetation come back; the first bend after the bridge is open, never blind.
Not this
A glass deck; a second arch or a second bridge; anything standing on the deck; towers rising from the ravine floor.

ZONE 4 · PK 2 181 to 2 826Plateaux: the high plateau and the tunnel

The road lands on the high shoulder at +215 m, passes a small pavilion set back on the left and a basin whose stream falls into the ravine on the right, climbs to +225 m and goes straight through a spur of P4, 75 m of rock above the road, in an 80 m tunnel. After it the cliff edge comes back to 12 m from the left barrier.

Mood
The calm before the drop: more intimate, a little water, then a cold dark gate in the mountain.
Landmark
The tunnel mouth in the face of the spur, with the exit visible through it as a bright dot.
Read at speed
  • the spur as one continuous striated mass across the road, P4 behind it on the right
  • a dark portal cut square in the rock, dressed stone round the mouth, the road in a short trench before it
  • a small red-brown pavilion with upturned eaves on a stone platform, tiny against the terrain
  • a turquoise basin and a narrow white fall on the inside, never crossing the road
  • pale stone and moss on the plateau, isolated pines at the edges, none at the portal
Light
Open and soft on the plateau; cold shade in the tunnel, the exit always visible; one accent of turquoise, one of red-brown.
Transition
Out of the tunnel the eye slides left to the outside of the mountain and the low cloud comes back before the descent.
Not this
A giant pagoda or a temple complex; the natural arch of Tianmen as the tunnel; a lake; water on the road; a long or curved tunnel.

ZONE 5 · PK 2 826 to 3 700Descente: down the buttress above the cloud

From +215 to about +165 m in long downhill bends on a continuous buttress: a 26 m rock wall on the right with P5 behind it, the cliff edge 12 m from the left barrier and the sea of clouds below. HERO is in view through every opening. The woods of zone 1 close in again from PK 3 700.

Mood
The big release: the height won is given back, fast and wide, with the cloud under the left wheels.
Landmark
The HERO needle, alone in the middle of the ravine; P5 as the nearer mass on the right.
Read at speed
  • a continuous faceted wall on the inside, warmer salmon sandstone than the rest of the lap
  • a stone parapet on the outside and the flat white cloud beyond it, below the wheels
  • needles of different heights across the ravine, paler with distance, feet in the cloud
  • vegetation thickening as the road loses height, groves returning on both sides
  • a buried culvert under the road (PK 3 290): a stone headwall, no water on the lane
Light
The same morning as zone 1: light salmon on the rock, blue-grey valley in layers.
Transition
The groves thicken, the horizon closes, the trunks at the barrier start again: the road flattens and meets the start line with no change of width.
Not this
Mist at road level; a parallel road or another tunnel; red desert rock; a gap in the parapet.
