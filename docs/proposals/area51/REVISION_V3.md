# AREA 51 — V3 vector plan

V3 replaces the V2 plan for placements. Main deliverable: `plan-area51-v3.svg`, fully vector and editable, with no image generation. `plan-area51-v3.html` also displays the same SVG in a browser.

## What changes

- Addition of an aviation domain in the north-east: runway A of 1,800 × 45 m, runway B of 1,100 × 30 m, taxiways, parking apron, two aviation hangars and a tower. Fiction dimensions for the game; no aeronautical compliance claimed.
- The winding five-zone loop is kept. The race does not go around an airfield rectangle and does not cross the aviation runways.
- Addition of a real passage through B1 in the hill, after H1. The bunker houses a through logistics gallery: two opposite portals, no dead end, side laboratories outside the drivable strip. It is no longer a decorative bunker at the roadside.
- Roofs are drawn in transparency, with covered road dotted and orange portals. Thin olive-grey lines designate services; the dark blue strip is the sole race route.

## Reference dimensions

Loop of **5,701.45 m** along the centreline, measured over 500 intervals per segment; 23 cubic segments. Closure verified and no self-intersection of the sampled centreline. Start in the west, clockwise. Race 20 m usable, widening planned to 24 m at the tests and braking zones; the line drawing remains symbolic. 530 m test line in the north-east.

**H1:** 130 × 90 m footprint, 130 m covered passage on a 270 m straight axis. East entrance at x=1,710 m, west exit at x=1,580 m; axis north=250 m. Straight reserves of 60 m at the entrance and 80 m at the exit. Doors 32 × 14 m, usable lane 20 m.

**B1:** 120 m covered passage on a 280 m straight axis. East entrance at x=1,320 m, west exit at x=1,200 m; axis north=400 m. Straight reserves of 80 m on each side. Free gallery 24 × 9 m, portals 28 × 10 m; drivable strip 20 m, continuous lighting, no pillar or stock on the roadway. Roof covered by the hill: the road stays at plateau level while the terrain rises above.

You must cross H1, go around the technical yard, line up, then cross B1: the two buildings do not form a single tunnel. The covered sections are at intermediate speed; their doors are not hidden braking zones.

## Site coherence and next steps

Radios stay in the west, workshops in the south-east, aviation in the north-east. Service roads link operations, antennas, workshops and aviation. Accesses crossing the race are closed during the event. The bunker has its transfer gallery; the experimentation rooms do not encroach on it.

V3 is a design plan and not a validated playable map. The V2 profile must not be applied automatically to the new centreline: keep the hangar, bunker and their approaches flat, recompute the vertical chainage during the mock-up. Then validate the curvature transitions, the two passages back to back and the visibility of the bunker exit.
