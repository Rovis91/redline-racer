# Area 51 / Signal Unknown: life and atmosphere, V3 specification

Research document for the implementation step. Nothing here is built. Method: `docs/methods/LIFE.md`; engine options: `docs/methods/MAP-ENGINE-OPTIONS.md`; mood: `AMBIANCE-V2.html` (same folder). Read the scenery in `src/maps/area51/` (placers are described at the top of `scenery-area51.js`).

How this was made: a racing-camera board every 60 samples (55 views, 5 boards, `node tools/plan.js area51 @0 @60 …`, net style, autopilot at about 115 km/h in the corners), the scenery code, the mood sheet and the shipped maps. Sample = 2 m of road (N = 3310, lap 6595 m). Fractions of the lap (what `AMBZ` and `ACOUSTICS.spots` use) = sample / 3310.

## 1. Summary: what changes the map most (priority order)

1. **A jet takes off on runway B once per lap, alongside the runway A sprint.** Spool-up heard from the taxiway, the aircraft accelerating ahead-left (260 m off), lifting off mid-sprint with a lit afterburner, then climbing into the violet. The map's one big "event". (Z5-01)
2. **Everything that listens turns together.** D1, D2-D4 and the five calibration dishes slew in unison on one shared drive; the two airfield radars scan; the tower beacon sweeps. "Antennas that rotate" with a story: they all slowly track the west. (Z3-01, Z3-02, Z5-03, Z5-04)
3. **The signal.** The amber glow above the western range pulses on a slow double beat, a sub-bass hum swells with it, the dishes occasionally "lock" on it. Never an object, never a switch. Visible on the ridge climb (about 6 s). (G-03, Z4-01, Z4-02)
4. **The reactor pool breathes.** Blue light on walls and vault that slowly pulses, moving caustics, polar crane creeping over the pool, a different acoustic space. The hall and B1 become rooms you can hear. (Z2-03 to Z2-07, G-02)
5. **A sound bed and a place for every zone.** Desert wind plus a faint mains hum everywhere, ballast hum in H1, reactor hum underground, wind in the lattice at the array, the tower chime on the airfield. All from `AMB` / `AMBZ` / `ACOUSTICS` / `SOUND.spots`. (G-01, G-02, one S row per zone)
6. **The base is switched on.** Red obstruction beacons on every mast and dish tip, sequenced "rabbit" flashers on the approach lights, light pools under the apron masts. One shared flash clock. (G-06, Z5-05, Z1-03)
7. **You are being watched.** A searchlight sweeping outward from a watchtower and a patrol pickup with an amber flasher running the depot lane. (Z4-03, Z4-04)
8. **The visitors are rare.** The eight grey figures show on a rotating subset of laps and two of them wave in two poses. (Z4-08)

### Sound bed (all in `src/audio/audio.js`, read-only for me; the implementer adds entries)

- `AMB.area51`: `[['bandpass', 380, 0.6, 0.035, 0.07, 0.6], ['lowpass', 120, 0.7, 0.06, 0.04, 0.4], ['bandpass', 100, 8, 0.012]]`. Desert wind, a low rumble of the base, and a very narrow band at 100 Hz that reads as mains hum.
- `ACOUSTICS.area51`: `{ slap: 0.13, sl: 0.08, intoWall: 0.3, rv: 0.04, dur: 1.8, lp: 2800, spots: [[0.045, 0.095, 5], [0.150, 0.272, 7]] }`. Open basin; the `spots` multiply the reverb send in H1 (samples 150-315) and in B1 (495-900). `dur` is one value per map: 1.8 s is a compromise that is long for the hall and still tolerable in the open at `rv 0.04`.
- Important: B1 is not an engine tunnel (`tun: null`), so `tun` is 0 there: the vault bed (`AMBZ { tun: 1 }`) and the tunnel convolver will not engage. Use `de` / `a` fractions for B1, as listed below. Wall echoes still follow `intoWall`.
- `AMBZ.area51` (fractions of the lap): H1 `{ de: 0.045, a: 0.095 }` ballast hum; B1 `{ de: 0.150, a: 0.272 }` reactor and ventilation; antenna field `{ de: 0.29, a: 0.50 }` wind in lattice; ridge `{ de: 0.47, a: 0.60 }` the signal hum (LFO 0.07 Hz matched to the glow pulse); airfield `{ de: 0.66, a: 0.97 }` wide quiet air.
- `ambEvent` for `area51` (a branch beside `chapada`, `touge`…): every 30 to 70 s a distant coyote or a short burst of radio static with no voice, never both within 20 s. Return the delay as the others do.
- `SOUND.spots('area51', [...])` for point sources, modelled on `scenery-barrage.js` line ~653. Existing sound names usable: `transformer`, `turbine`, `whoosh`, `loudspeaker`, `train`, `crusher`. New names needed: `jet`, `servo`.

## 3. Reading the lap: zone by zone

Row key: **Cat** SND sound, LIGHT light ambience, FOG fog or haze, DYNL dynamic light (`h.emis`), REFL reflection, WATER water, OBJ dynamic object, PART particle. **Build** starts with `REUSE:` (existing code to copy, file and function named) or `NEW:` (precise description). **Pri** P0 map needs it, P1 identity, P2 nice. Durations are at lap speed: about 35 m/s on the base, 60 m/s on runway A. Everything that moves carries `userData.dyn = 1` and is welded in its own group (`weld(h, grp, fam)`, wangan `ship()` pattern) or the scenery merge freezes it.

## 4. What NOT to do on this map

- **No wet look through the engine** (`wetVisible`, `damp`): the map lays its own roadway ribbon, which will not show it, and a `damp` zone changes the grip the sim computes.
- **No visible UFO, saucer, beam, ray, or object in the glow.** The mood sheet says never. The glow pulses; it does not show a cause.
- **No animation at the hairpin or any chevron.** The telemetry dish (310, 560) stays still. The windsock is the only moving thing near a braking board, small and gentle. The approach rabbit stops before the corner at sample 2302.
- **No aircraft crossing the race road or the runway A strip.** The takeoff is on runway B. A plane crossing the race is an anti-reference of the mood sheet.
- **No sun movement, no day cycle.** The sun disc is a blue-hour fixture on the fan.
- **No vegetation sway, no heat shimmer, no soft smoke.** ART-STYLE: no shimmer, no soft modern effects. Sage and Joshua stay merged and still; steam is `h.steam`.
- **No moving collidable objects**; every mover is visual only (LIFE.md; pickup, tug, aircraft, crane).
- **No beam that can cross the camera** (wangan note). Searchlight arc stays outward.
- **No effect out of view**: nothing on the far hangar roofs, the fuel farm, the back shops, the water tower, the far ranges.
- **No tunnel features** (`th.tun`) just to get eye adaptation: B1 is a scenery shell and the engine tunnel would fight it.
- **No hangar door animation** and no jet bridge: H1's gable doors are open permanently ("huge doors left open").
- **No leaves** (`th.leaves`): no trees on the map.
- **No tram.** There is no tram shape; a moving cart on the goods siding would be one more moving thing in a 4 s window.

## 5. Open questions for the author

1. **Takeoff every lap, or on a schedule?** Recommended: every lap, alternating SR-71 and F-117, two aircraft every third lap. A takeoff every second lap would make it a rarer treat.
2. **Do you accept a short, ramped blink near the runway crossing (Z5-12) and the approach rabbit (Z5-06)?** The rule says no blink at a braking point; the rabbit is the only classic airfield cue and it ends before the corner.
3. **Should the signal ever answer the dishes?** Today the dishes "lock" and the glow surges together every 3 to 4 minutes. Recommended; the alternative is a purely independent pulse.
4. **Hall and B1 floor reflections:** streaks of light only (Z1-04), or do you want the engine's real wet look tested on the hall at the cost of a changed grip there? Recommended: streaks only.
5. **Tram:** do you want a tram built in B1 after all (the V2 comment names one)? Recommended: no.
