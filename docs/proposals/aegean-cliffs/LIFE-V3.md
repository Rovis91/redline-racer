# Aegean Cliffs (`egee`): life and atmosphere, V3

Research document for the implementation step. No game code was written. Read with `docs/methods/LIFE.md`, `docs/methods/MAP-ENGINE-OPTIONS.md`, `docs/ART-STYLE.md` and `docs/proposals/aegean-cliffs/AMBIANCE-V2.html`.

**Units.** PK = plan metres from the start line (0 to 4700, the unit of `scenery-egee*.js`). Sample = PK / 1.993 (2358 samples). Control point (`M.pts`, used by `th.haze`, `th.seaSpray`, `surf`) = PK / 40 (one point every 40 m, 117 points). Audio fractions (`AMBZ` `de`/`a`) = PK / 4700 = sample / 2358. Lap speed: `spd: [110, 175]` km/h = 30 to 49 m/s; "seen for N s" below assumes 40 m/s. Side: -1 island, +1 sea.
**Category codes.** S sound, L light/sky/shadow, F fog/haze, D dynamic light, R reflection, W water, O dynamic object, P particle. **Priority.** P0 the map needs it, P1 identity, P2 nice to have. **Build.** REUSE (file, function) or NEW (described). **Risk.** shimmer / flicker, road readability, cost.

## What was looked at

- Docs above; `src/data/maps.js` entry (line 739 onward); every file of `src/maps/egee/`; `src/audio/audio.js` (`AMB`, `AMBZ`, `ACOUSTICS`, `spots`); the barrage, plage, wangan, chapada code named in the rows.
- Captures: `node tools/plan.js egee lapRun --style=game` (20 shots, every 118 samples, two boards) plus one extra board of 12 shots at samples 30, 240, 360, 470, 1060, 1180, 1300, 1420, 1540, 1650, 1890, 2120 (`captures/plans/egee/`, git-ignored). Autopilot speed in the shots is 88 to 100 km/h, slower than a player; durations below use 40 m/s.
- The brief asked for one view every 60 samples. The tool's lap run gives one every 118, so the extra board fills the zones that matter (windmills, Balcon, harbour). Gaps remain; see "What could not be verified".

## What the player actually sees today (all static)

- 35 to 45 % of every frame is empty blue sky (no clouds, no birds), and 15 to 30 % is sea on the right, flat and unmoving, almost the whole lap. Those two areas are where motion is cheapest and most noticed.
- A plane of sea at y = 6: `seaTurquoise` (opacity 0.6, rep 1200, nearest filter) over `seaWide`; a sun-halo patch already sits on the horizon when the road faces the sun (shots 1592, 1890).
- 25 `kaikiEgee` boats (9 on the pontoons, 4 along the quay, up to 16 in the bay), 6 islets, 6 far islands, all still. 4 windmills (3 on the plateau PK 2190 to 2290, one on the Balcon PK 2900 at scale 1.5) with sails frozen. Lighthouse lantern is an emissive box, not lit by anything.
- No flag, no bunting, no laundry, no smoke, no bird anywhere on the map. `scenery-egee.js` returns no `tick`.
- Audio: `amb: []` in the map entry and **no `AMB.egee`, `AMBZ.egee`, `ACOUSTICS.egee`**: the map is silent apart from the car (`ACOUSTICS.attempt` fallback).
- Power wires are the only line crossing the sky; in motion they read well and need nothing.

## Summary: what changes the map most (priority order)

1. **Sound ambience from zero** (G1, G2 and the five zone beds): sea wash and wind everywhere, cicadas on the dry slopes, harbour clinking, chapel bell and goat bells on the crest. Today the map is silent; this is the biggest gain per hour.
2. **Windmills that turn** (3.1, 4.1): four sail wheels, slow and out of phase, with a soft thump per passing sail. The signature motion of the island and an author request.
3. **The sea moves** (G5, G6, 1.2): slow double-layer scroll of the sea plane with linear filtering, a sun-glitter strip under the sun that follows the car, foam strip on the quay wall, foam rings round the islets. Sea fills the right of the frame all lap.
4. **Boats bob and one boat sails** (1.1, 1.9): 13 harbour boats and the near bay boats rock on two axes, all heading into the same wind; one fishing boat leaves the basin with a wake.
5. **Birds** (1.5, 2.2, 3.8, 4.4, 5.5): gulls over the basin and gliding below the cornice, perched gulls that scatter on approach on the quay, doves bursting out of the two dovecotes on the plateau. Fills the sky and gives one-second events.
6. **Wind made visible** (1.4, 3.6, 3.7, G3): flags and bunting on the quay front and the chapel square, laundry at the plateau houses, all streaming along one wind vector (given in G3).
7. **A sky with clouds and crisper light** (G4, G7): a few drifting flat cumulus, `th.shadows: { soft: 1 }` for the lime-and-stone contrast, optional halo trim.
8. **A far ferry** (4.3): one white ship crossing the open sea beyond the Balcon, with the wangan horn event, the longest open view of the lap.

## Reflections and light

No `th.wetVisible` (dry island; `surf` damp zones would also change driving). `th.gloss: 0.02` stays (the road must not mirror the sun). The only reflection work is the sun glitter G6 and the soft shadows G4. Do not add mirror geometry for the lighthouse or the boats.

## What NOT to do on this map, with the reason

- **Rain, wet road, lens spray (`th.seaSpray`, `th.wetVisible`).** Midday meltemi in July: the mood sheet is hard white light. Lens drops read as rain; the cliff is 120 m above the sea, spray would not reach the road.
- **Dust and leaves (`th.dust`, `th.leaves`).** The engine emits dust only on `surf` zones that have a `rollAmt` (`game.js` lines 828 to 834); egee has none, and adding one changes physics ("what you see is what you drive"). Not worth a sim change for a trail.
- **Lights and glow (`h.emis`, a lit lantern, harbour lamps, shrine candles, a flashing beacon).** Nothing glows at 14:30; light that appears in full sun flips like a switch (ART-STYLE). The lighthouse keeps its self-lit `feu` box.
- **Time-of-day drift across the lap** (sun lowering, colour shift). 45 s to 70 s is too short; ART-STYLE forbids light that flips.
- **Tree sway by shader.** No shader clock exists (LIFE.md "To build" 2) and `illuminates` replaces `onBeforeCompile`: it would cost a shared engine change for an effect seen at a pixel. Olive and cypress stay still.
- **Heat shimmer on the asphalt.** Shimmer is the first thing ART-STYLE tells us to avoid, and the road must stay readable.
- **Sailing yachts, ferries by the dozen, jet-skis, a crowd of swimmers.** The mood sheet's "not this": a resort or a marina. One ferry (4.3) and one fishing boat (1.3) only.
- **Any moving thing within 30 m of a corner entry or a chevron** (LIFE.md rule). Flags (1.4) and doves (3.5) are placed on straights; check `chevK 0.014` once.
- **Colliding or race-clock-dependent hazards.** All movers are pure scenery. The only race-clock dependency is the paths of the boat and the ferry (use `h.time()` so online viewers agree).
- **Lowering `fogD` or adding thick haze.** The bay's colour is the map's value: `th.haze` only as the light P2 touch of 4.8.
- **Animating the awnings, scooter, pick-ups or café parasols.** They are 1-second glimpses, merged into `kit`, and animating them breaks the weld for little gain.

## Open questions for the author

1. **Sound character.** Zoned beds (cicadas on slopes, harbour in the port) are proposed as P0. Are cicadas acceptable all across the dry slopes (they are loud), or only on the plateau and the Descente?
2. **Race-day dressing.** The mood sheet promises bunting and a few stands, and the static map has none. Is the bunting and flag set (1.4, 3.6) a go, and should stands and a crowd with a wave at the start and the square be scoped as scenery first (`PLACERS.crowd` is for tiered stands, `src/render/crowd.js`)?
3. **Glitter.** G6 is the riskiest shimmer item. Do you want it tried first and cut if flickery, or skipped in favour of the horizon halo already visible?
4. **Ferry.** One ferry crossing the Balcon view with a horn: does a ferry fit the "working island" mood, or should the only moving vessel be fishing boats?
5. **Clouds.** The V2 sheet says clear midday with a pale horizon band. Are a few flat drifting cumulus allowed, or does the sky stay empty?

## What could not be verified

- Wind vector G3 comes from the boats' authored `rotation.y`, not from the lap geometry; not checked against a board.
- Sound levels, the `de`/`a` edge behaviour and the names of existing `SOUNDS` entries were read in `audio.js` but not heard; all sounds named here are new synthesis.
- Whether the engine casts shadows now (none is set in `th`) is unknown from the boards; the shadow rows depend on it.
- The 60-sample frame-by-frame pass was replaced by 32 views (20 at 118 samples plus 12 chosen); stretches between them were read from the code. No motion capture (`--burst`) was run.
