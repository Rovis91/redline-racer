# AEGEAN CLIFFS — Art direction and references

Preproduction, 16 November 2026. Fictional island inspired by Santorini and the Cyclades: no real distance or altitude is transposed. The asset dimensions below are **proposed templates**, not surveys. The computed geometry of the master file is authoritative for the roadway, its sectors and its altitude.

## Intent and territory

A sheltered port, a service road that reaches the village over a rocky shoulder, then a cornice and a descent down the opposite slope. The village is built around the available terraces; the 12–16 m road follows its fringe and an open square, not its genuine pedestrian alleys. Small stairs stay off-track. The port has a working quay and a breakwater; avoid the big luxury marina.

The white silhouette of the village and its blue chapel form **one dominant landmark**. Three secondary landmarks: breakwater, windmill on an exposed ridge, rocky promontory. The windmill stays near the village and the old agricultural terraces, which gives it a function beyond decor.

The sources describe Santorini as a volcanic landscape with villages on the caldera, and Mykonos as a different island, notably granitic. We borrow the Cycladic volumes and windmills without randomly mixing all geologies: for this island, layered beige tuff cliffs, rust levels and a dark base. [GNTO, volcanoes](https://www.visitgreece.gr/en/experiences/nature/volcanoes/the-volcanoes-of-santorini); [Ephorate of Cyclades, Mykonos](https://cyclades.culture.gov.gr/en/location/mykonos/).

## Five zones: gameplay framings

| Zone | Driving | Composition and transition | Main assets |
|---|---|---|---|
| Port | Start, slipstream, nitro and overtaking | Open foreground; village glimpsed above the cliff. Quay on the sea side, warehouses on the land side. | Quay, caiques, low shed, lamps, breakwater |
| Ascent | Announced braking, wide hairpins and re-acceleration | Lowered inner embankment around the entries; continuous outer low wall. The village grows progressively. | Layered cliffs, retaining walls, bollards, arrows |
| Village | Precision without a chokepoint | Facades tangent to the route, clear square before the exit. The chapel emerges from the roofs. | Houses, dome, terraces, stairs, bougainvillea |
| Balcony | Large curves and prolonged drift | Main reveal: sea and port beneath the player. Windmill set back from the apex, not in front of the view. | Parapet, windmill, dry terraces, islet silhouette |
| Descent | Re-accelerations, fast positioning then return to flat | The sea returns to eye level; port buildings announce the final braking. | Low walls, low rocks, sparse vegetation, port beacons |

## Visual mini-bible

- **Keywords:** bright, mineral, tiered, windy, maritime.
- **Seven-colour palette:** lime `#F4EEDC`, dome blue `#2067AD`, deep sea `#154E73`, coastal water `#44B7BB`, tuff `#C5AA83`, asphalt `#3D4148`, flowers `#C95583`. The rust strata use a desaturated variation of the tuff.
- **Proposed lighting:** late-morning sun, high and from the side; short readable shadows, slightly cream whites. No overexposure of facades or continuous white reflection on the asphalt.
- **Shapes and materials:** low cubic volumes, small vaults, faceted dome, rock in large strata. Lime, volcanic stone, matte asphalt, painted wood, water. Coarse textures repeated at low density; avoid photo grain and tiny joints.
- **Game style:** camera behind an angular car, coherent 3D projection, flat colour fills and visible pixel textures, simplified silhouettes. Local references examined: the 12-track overview image, `raceCase-1.png` and a Paris style capture. Thumbnails must show a roadway as readable as in those captures; do not become tourist paintings or Unreal renders.
- **Density:** medium port, very airy ascent, dense village but outside the road gauge, almost empty balcony, moderately vegetated descent. Detail concentrates on the outer side of the lines and near landmarks.
- **Water:** turquoise band in the small coves and around the quay; deep blue offshore. The colour change indicates plausible depth, without a tropical lagoon ringing the whole island.
- **Movement and sound:** slow windmill sails, a banner at the port, short swell, distant birds; occasional bell in the village, wind on the cornice. No effect hides the apex.
- **Three prohibitions:** 90° alleys as the dominant driving; palms/jungle/pools everywhere; architecture, flowers or shadows that hide a corner entry.

## Points to keep for the blockout

The wide road in the village is an explicit arcade liberty: a peripheral service road and a square, never a supposed 14 m historic alley. Buildings must be set on real terraces of the blockout, with bases and retaining walls. Views and visibility margins will be checked in the game camera; this documentary research does not demonstrate them.
