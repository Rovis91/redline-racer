# LUNAR OUTPOST — standing user rule for concept art

From the V2 revision of 17 November 2026, all depictions of the player vehicle use a **small closed-cabin lunar car**: compact buggy, four visible wheels, angular body, solid rigid roof, very dark opaque windows. No player, driver or passenger visible. No open cockpit, convertible or simple roll bar without a roof. Stop depicting the player vehicle as an ordinary road sports car.

Proposed visual standard: 3.5 m long, 2.1 m wide, 1.8 m high. Ivory body, small orange marking, black tyres, low-poly geometry and pixelated textures. This concept-art convention requires no change to the game physics or any playable 3D model at this stage.

The [V2 board](v2/planche-lunar-outpost.png) and the V2 folder (`v2/`) replace V1 as the design reference. V1 is kept for comparison.
