Sunken Highway

Mood and reference sheet, V2. Written after the blockout was validated; it follows the zones of the built map (src/maps/autoroute/), not the layout of the V2 bible, and keeps the bible's art direction.

A fictional coastal city, evacuated in a hurry and left to itself for decades. The race is not on a track: it is the one way still open through real streets, because every other street is closed by something that happened there. You drive through a city, not around a circuit: every bend has a reason you can see.

Place
A street grid of 110 m blocks. The boulevard runs east to west through the office district; the ground falls gently east to the river, where the warehouse district stands in shallow water; a motorway crosses the city on a viaduct above one street; a metro line runs under another.
Time and light
Golden hour, late afternoon, one weather for the whole lap. Low sun from the west: long shadows across the streets, orange facades on one side and blue-grey on the other, teal water in contrast. Under the motorway and in the metro the light drops, never to black.
Style
Retro low-poly: simple volumes, modular facades, pixel textures, vegetation as bold silhouettes. Realism lives in the composition and in what each place tells, not in detail. Read at 150 km/h before being read up close.
Story
Environmental, as in The Last of Us (the author's references from the game and the series are shown below). People fled: traffic stands in its lanes facing the way out, the army closed streets, buildings fell later. Nature came back from where it already was: the planted trees, the park, the water.
Anti-references
A track laid in a ruined set (guardrails, chevrons, kerbs, tyre walls, a uniform racing surface); wrecks arranged as a slalom; ruin spread evenly everywhere; a city wholly under water; jungle hiding the next bend; rain and night; photorealistic clutter; any on-screen interface in the mood views.

THE VIBEThe author's references

The look to reach, from the images the author supplied. They set the mood, the light and the density of vegetation and wrecks; the game keeps its own low-poly, pixel-textured style and copies none of them.

THE WHOLE LAPWhat makes it a city and not a track

The road
No guardrail, no chevron, no cone, no racing line painted. The edge of the road is a kerb, a facade, a parapet, a platform edge. The asphalt is patched, cracked and stained differently street by street; lane markings survive in places only.
Closed streets
At every crossroads the streets not taken are visibly closed, each by its own event: an army barrage, a fallen facade, an overturned bus, a fallen tree, a stranded barge, a subsided roadway. Never the same one twice in a row.
Buildings
Built from a small kit of parts (ground floor, repeated storey, cornice or roof, corner), assembled block by block so that no two blocks are the same. Kind follows the district: office towers of 45 to 110 m on the boulevard, brick housing of 28 to 55 m to the south, workshops to the north, low warehouses by the river.
Vegetation
It has an origin. Street trees grown huge where they were planted; the park become a wood that spills onto its pavements; ivy on the facades that take the afternoon sun; reeds and willows where water stands; grass in the joints where traffic has stopped. Nothing at random.
Vehicles
Mid-size sedans, pickups, vans, buses, a few army trucks, all of one period. Rusted, flat tyres, some doors open, weeds through the bonnets. Grouped where a jam, a checkpoint or an accident would have put them; whole stretches have none.

ZONE 1The boulevard

The start straight, heading east: 26 m between kerbs, open from kerb to kerb except at three places. An army checkpoint, a facade fallen from the north side, a pile-up packed against the south kerb, then under the motorway viaduct to the buried end of the boulevard.

Mood
The main street on the day everyone left: the checkpoint that filtered them, and what has fallen since.
Landmark
The motorway viaduct crossing the boulevard 20 m up, seen from the start line between two rows of trees.
Read at speed
  • two rows of huge street trees on the pavements, gold on the sunlit side
  • the checkpoint: staggered lines of concrete barriers, a watchtower, an olive truck and tent
  • a slope of pale rubble spilling from a gutted office front, taller towards the building
  • a tight block of rusted cars and a bus lying askew, on one side only
  • the dark underside and piers of the viaduct, then a wall of debris closing the street ahead
Light
Sun behind the car at the start: the street glows ahead, long tree shadows fall across it. A band of cold shade under the viaduct.
Transition
The boulevard is buried at its east end. The only open street turns north and the ground gets wet.
Not this
Cars lining both sides of the road from end to end; obstacles alternating left and right at regular intervals; a clean tarmac ribbon between them.

ZONE 2The warehouse district

Six street corners on the low ground by the river: north, east, north, west, north, west. Narrow streets of 16 m between low warehouses, every street not driven standing in shallow water.

Mood
Quiet and reflective: the only part of the city the water has taken, calm enough to mirror the facades.
Landmark
A barge stranded across a street, seen at the end of the first straight.
Read at speed
  • long low warehouses in grey-blue sheet metal and brick, loading doors, faded painted signs
  • teal water at the kerb on both sides, the road dry and slightly domed
  • reeds and young willows in the flooded side streets, a tide line on the walls
  • at each corner one closed arm: barge, fallen tree, collapsed quay, tanker truck
  • cranes and silos over the roofs towards the river
Light
Streets running north are in shade with a bright sky strip; streets running east and west take the low sun full length and the water throws it back.
Transition
The last street ends under the motorway at an army barrage: the only way on is the slip road, up.
Not this
Water on the driven road; a lagoon with no streets; tropical jungle.

ZONE 3The motorway

The slip road climbs along the east side and merges at +12 m. Two carriageways of 14 m either side of a central reservation: the east one is jammed ahead, the lane crosses a break in the reservation to the west one, over the summit at +20 m above the boulevard, then down the last span, which has fallen to the street.

Mood
The way out that nobody got out by, and the one place where you see the whole city.
Landmark
From the summit: the towers of the boulevard, the park as a green mass, the river and its warehouses in the sun.
Read at speed
  • a concrete central reservation with weeds, torn open at one place
  • the other carriageway full of cars in their lanes, all facing out of the city
  • a bus and a jackknifed truck lying across at the break
  • plain concrete parapets, sign gantries with faded green panels, lamp posts leaning
  • the last span tilted down as a ramp, its broken edge and bare reinforcement
Light
The brightest place of the lap: open sky, sun from the right, the city in haze below. No shade at all until the fallen span.
Transition
The fallen span lands in a street of brick housing where every way is closed but one.
Not this
Vehicles set out as a slalom; a broken gap to jump; a modern glass-and-steel bridge.

ZONE 4The fallen street

A short street heading west between brick housing blocks. It ends on a ledge: the roadway has fallen into the metro, and the car drops 11 m in free fall onto the tracks, in a shaft open to the sky.

Mood
A dead end, then the ground gives way.
Landmark
The dark rectangle of the hole across the whole street, with the buried street beyond it.
Read at speed
  • broken roadway slabs tilting at the ledge, bent rails and cables hanging
  • the cut edge of the street in section: asphalt, fill, pipes, the tunnel roof
  • two metro trains crushed either side of the landing
  • brick facades with fire escapes rising straight above the shaft
  • a shaft of low sun falling into the hole
Light
Warm street, then a sudden drop into cool shade with one bright slab of sunlight on the tracks.
Transition
The tunnel mouth, straight ahead under the buried street.
Not this
A gentle ramp; a hole the player could choose to avoid; a natural cave.

ZONE 5The metro

A straight double-track tunnel under the street, the lane passing from one track to the other around stalled trains. At the station a train stands at the platform: up a heap of rubble onto the platform, along it, into the passenger corridor and up the stairs to the street. The tunnel itself goes on west, closed by a derailed train. Then one street north, back to the boulevard.

Mood
Still and damp, lit by what is left of the emergency lighting; you leave the way a passenger would.
Landmark
The station: the train at the platform, and daylight coming down the stairs.
Read at speed
  • a rectangular concrete tunnel, columns between the two tracks, cable trays on the walls
  • trains as long pale boxes with dark window bands, one pushed off its track
  • the platform edge strip, tiled walls, a station name panel, benches
  • turnstiles and a ticket booth at the mouth of the corridor
  • roots and ferns where the stairwell lets light in, water dripping, never standing on the lane
Light
Green-white emergency lights at regular intervals, no black holes; the stairwell is a warm rectangle that grows as you climb. Coming out, the sun is in the west, to the left.
Transition
The stairs come up under a station canopy on a side street; the boulevard and its trees are in view at the next crossroads, and the lap closes on the checkpoint.
Not this
Wide sweeping tunnel bends; a flooded tunnel; a cave; a clean modern station.
