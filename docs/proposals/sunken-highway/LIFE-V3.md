# Sunken Highway: life and atmosphere, V3 specification

Research document for the implementation step. Nothing here is built. Method: `docs/methods/LIFE.md`; engine options: `docs/methods/MAP-ENGINE-OPTIONS.md`; mood: `AMBIANCE-V2.html` (same folder). Scenery read: `src/maps/autoroute/` (zone files `scenery-autoroute-{boulevard,quartier,pont,trou,metro,ville}.js`, placers and `real()` in `scenery-autoroute.js`), map entry `id === 'autoroute'` in `src/data/maps.js`.

How this was made: `node tools/plan.js autoroute lapRun --style=game` (20 racing-camera views, one every 70 samples, 2 boards) plus 12 targeted views (`@300 @340 @560 @625 @690 @890 @915 @1245 @1265 @1285 @1320 @1345`), the scenery code, the mood sheet, the author's eight references (`local/sunken-highway/`, not re-read: the mood sheet quotes them) and the shipped maps. The brief asked for one view every 60 samples; I used 70 plus the targeted ones (same coverage, fewer renders on a 16 GB machine). Browser closed afterwards.

Numbers used below: N = 1400 samples, lap about 2770 m (1 sample about 2 m), the autopilot drives 60 to 100 km/h, so a lap is about 51 s and one sample is about 37 ms. Fractions of the lap (what `AMBZ` and `ACOUSTICS.spots` use) = sample / 1400. Sample positions are derived from the arc length of the path (`pts`, K indices from `maps.js`) and checked against the boards; they are good to about 10 samples. Key points (M.pts index / sample): `depart` 0 / 0, `eauA` 26 / 312, `eauB` 51 / 574, `rampe` 55 / 613, `fusion` 63 / 668, `travee` 76 / 837, `sol` 79 / 879, `bord` 84 / 921, `bouche` 90 / 968, `station` 112 / 1275, `portail` 118 / 1344, `tremie` 120 / 1364. Platform about 1245-1295.

Two facts that shape everything:

1. **The scenery has no `tick` and nothing carries `userData.dyn` on this map today.** Everything below is added. The map is one welded `kit` (`weld(h, kit)` at the end of `scenery-autoroute.js`): anything that moves must be built outside the kit (own mesh, `userData.dyn = 1`) or it is frozen into it.
2. **The route is mostly a street with facades 17 to 24 m either side.** What the player sees is a corridor of about 40 to 70 m width and a sky strip. Life has to be in that corridor, on the facades, or in the sky strip; a field of animals or a far skyline is invisible except from the summit (samples 690-800) and the river end of the boulevard.

## 1. Summary: what changes the map most (priority order)

1. **The water is alive and mirrors the street (flooded district, samples 312-574).** The water plane drifts east on the wind, the car makes a bow wave and a wake, and the 14 m lane under 30 cm of water reflects the facades and the low sun through the engine's wet-road pass. This is the map where water matters most. (Z2-01, Z2-02, Z2-03, G-04)
2. **The hole breathes.** One slanted sun shaft into the shaft of the collapsed street with dust turning in it, a broken main pouring down the south bank, cables swinging and doves lifting out of the opening as the car lands. (Z4-01 to Z4-05)
3. **The metro is a deep acoustic space with emergency light.** Reverb and a low room tone, a green lamp that dips and recovers (never a switch), two daylight shafts from stairwell grates, damp haze, drips, and the stairwell light growing on the ramp. (Z5-01 to Z5-08, G-02, G-03)
4. **Birds say "nobody here".** Doves on the checkpoint barriers, egrets standing in the flood, a flock on the first sign gantry of the motorway, all lifting on approach, all existing code. (Z1-01, Z2-06, Z3-02, Z4-05)
5. **A city with no engines sounds like one.** Wind in the towers, leaf rustle, water lap, wind whistling in bars on the summit, a metro drone; occasional structural creaks and distant birds. Six zone beds, all config in `audio.js`. (G-01 to G-03 and the S rows)
6. **Golden hour is lit properly.** Soft shadow edges (`shadows.soft`), three small haze zones that separate the city into layers, eye adaptation so the drop into the tube and the exit up the ramp fade. (G-05, G-06, Z2-09, Z3-04, Z5-05)
7. **Everything cloth, tin and cable moves on one wind** (from the west, blowing east): tent canvas and watchtower flag at the checkpoint, tarpaulin on the jack-knifed lorry, hanging cables, reeds, street-tree canopies. (Z1-04, Z3-06, Z4-04, Z2-04, G-07)
8. **Warm dust and lifted leaves.** `th.dust` + `th.leaves` give the car a trail of dead leaves on dry stretches and spray in the flooded stretch, for almost no code. (G-04)

### Sound bed (all in `src/audio/audio.js`, read-only for me; the implementer adds entries)

Entry format is `[type, freq, q, level, lfoRate, lfoDepth]` (see `AMB` and `AMBZ` there). Beds live under `V.amb`, scaled by the engine's own mix.

- `AMB.autoroute`: `[['bandpass', 480, 0.6, 0.03, 0.06, 0.6], ['lowpass', 110, 0.7, 0.04, 0.04, 0.4], ['highpass', 5200, 0.7, 0.005, 0.13, 0.9]]`. Wind among tall buildings, a deep city murmur with nothing in it, and a very faint insect band swelling and fading (chapada-style). No motor hum, no rain.
- `ACOUSTICS.autoroute`: `{ slap: 0.05, sl: 0.25, intoWall: 0.9, rv: 0.1, dur: 1.5, lp: 3600, spots: [[0.658, 0.691, 3.5], [0.889, 0.925, 4.5]] }`. Facades both sides give a quick slap (compare `ville` 0.034 / `paris` 0.028); `spots` multiply the reverb send in the cave (samples 921-968) and on the station platform (1245-1295). The tube is an engine tunnel (`tun: [bouche, portail]`), so the tunnel convolver engages by itself there.
- `AMBZ.autoroute` (fractions of the lap; the boulevard wraps the start line, so two entries):
  - boulevard `{ de: 0.974, a: 1 }` and `{ de: 0, a: 0.223 }`: `[['bandpass', 520, 0.6, 0.03, 0.07, 0.6], ['highpass', 5200, 0.7, 0.006, 0.13, 0.9]]` leaves and far birds.
  - flooded district `{ de: 0.223, a: 0.41 }`: `[['highpass', 3800, 0.6, 0.008, 0.25, 0.8], ['bandpass', 900, 0.9, 0.02, 0.4, 0.6], ['lowpass', 200, 0.7, 0.04, 0.1, 0.5]]` water lapping against walls, a low hollow note.
  - slip road and motorway `{ de: 0.41, a: 0.628 }`: `[['bandpass', 300, 0.5, 0.06, 0.05, 0.8], ['bandpass', 1800, 12, 0.01, 0.15, 0.9]]` open wind plus a narrow whistle in the bars (the second line is the whistle).
  - hole `{ de: 0.628, a: 0.691 }`: `[['highpass', 3200, 0.5, 0.03], ['bandpass', 1500, 0.8, 0.03, 0.9, 0.4]]` running water (the same pair as the `perse` garden), plus a dropped wind.
  - metro `{ tun: 1 }` (as `perse` bazaar): `[['lowpass', 90, 0.7, 0.08, 0.05, 0.4], ['bandpass', 600, 1.5, 0.012, 0.1, 0.5], ['highpass', 6500, 0.5, 0.004]]` room tone, a faint mid hum, a hiss of air.
  - ramp and forecourt `{ de: 0.96, a: 0.974 }`: the boulevard bed again, so the wind returns as the daylight does.
- Events (`ambEvent`, `touge` crickets pattern at `audio.js` about line 637): a dove coo (low sine 300 to 260 Hz wobble, 0.6 s), a crow (short band-passed noise burst with a downward formant), a structural creak (a 0.8 s bandpass sweep 180 to 120 Hz, low level, rare, only in `pont` and `quartier` zones), a drip (a 20 ms sine at 1800 to 2600 Hz, with the tunnel convolver, 1 to 3 s apart, metro only), a wind gust (a 3 s slow swell of the bed). Never regular, never two at the same time.

## 4. What NOT to do on this map

- **No rain, no snow, no night and no storm.** The mood is one weather: golden hour. `th.seaSpray` is the only lens effect and it is P2 because it can read as rain.
- **No full-screen fog.** The `th.haze` zones are three short bands. Do not raise `fogD` globally: the far ring of towers (`isFar 4000`) is the landmark from the summit and the boulevard.
- **No second layer or counter-moving layer on the water.** ART-STYLE bans shimmer; check with `--burst=6` and drop to a still texture if the single layer crawls.
- **No moving things on the lane.** No animal, no tumbling debris and no swinging object where the player drives: they read as obstacles. Animals stand or fly off beside the lane.
- **No fire, smoke columns or explosions.** The city has been empty for decades; nothing burns. A traffic light still powered is the only electric thing.
- **No deer or dogs as events on the road.** They would look like a collision and are not synchronised online.
- **No wind in the vine and ivy quads or the facade creeper.** They are cut-out quads welded into the kit (`quad('vine')`); animating them means unwelding hundreds. The tree and reed sway carries the wind.
- **No sparkle noise on the water.** The glint is one stretched patch from the wet-road pass, not particles.
- **No lit window animation in the towers.** Sunlight is behind them and it would read as people. The single exception is the train window in Z5-11.
- **No water surface in the metro.** The mood says "never standing on the lane": drips, seep streaks and edge puddles only.
- **No effect past the 60 m of view in a street.** Facades block it; the summit and the river end of the boulevard are the only long views.
- **No new shader for water, reflection or wind.** Everything above reuses texture offsets, matrices set in the tick, `h.emis`, `th.*` options or `softPuffs`. If Z2-03 fails, the fallback is a fixed additive glint quad, not a shader.

## 5. Open questions for the author

1. **The metro trains.** `rameAutoroute` (the train shape) exists but no scenery places it, so the station has no train and the cave has no crushed trains, although the mood sheet promises both. Do you want them placed first (a scenery task, not life), before I count Z5-11 and the platform's lit window?
2. **Flooded lane reflection.** Z2-03 requires removing the map's asphalt ribbon between points 26 and 51 so the engine's wet road shows. That changes the road's look in the flood (darker, a little shinier) and costs about 0.3 ms. Do you want it, or would you rather keep the flat green water and have only the sun glint quad?
3. **Water spray and the wake.** Is a wake behind the car (Z2-02, quads) wanted on top of the engine's spray, or do you want the spray alone?
4. **Animals.** Deer behind the park wall (Z1-05) are barely visible at lap speed. Keep a deer, or put all the budget in birds?
5. **Sparks in the cave.** A cable that sparks (Z4-04) is a blink in a spot where the player is falling. Keep it, or cables that only swing?

## 6. Not verified

- I rendered the 20 racing views and 12 targeted views of the static map, but I did not test any effect: nothing here has been built or seen in motion. Shimmer (water drift, canopy sway, falls) is unproven and must be checked with `--burst=6`.
- Sample positions come from the arc length of the path, not from the engine's own sample index; they are good to about 10 samples. Re-measure with `tools/plan.js autoroute @<sample>` before writing the `AMBZ` fractions.
- I did not read where `th.dust` fires in `src/engine/game.js` on a map with no dust surfaces; Z2-02 and G-04 assume it only acts in `damp` zones and on `th.leaves`.
- I did not verify that the screen-space reflection reflects the welded `kit` meshes (Z2-03); it works on shipped rain maps, but this map has a scenery asphalt ribbon over the road and a transparent water plane above it.
- I read the audio entries' format and the pattern of the existing beds, not their mix levels; all `AMB` values are starting points to tune by ear (`pages/listen.html`).
- I did not measure frame cost; the brief says to develop first and bench after.
